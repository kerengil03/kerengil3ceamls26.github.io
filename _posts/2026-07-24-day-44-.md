  Today, our group spent the day practicing and rehearsing for the Final Symposium on Sunday. Our mentors provided helpful feedback on areas we could improve before the symposium, and we made small adjustments to strengthen our presentation. By the end of the day, everyone felt much more prepared and confident. Overall, today's rehearsal helped us get ready for the Final Symposium and made me feel excited to present all of the work we accomplished this summer.
